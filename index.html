Regular · The lock

Collect all four glowing code fragments in the maze and reach the red exit. Solve its quick check, defeat the boss outside, find the car key, then get to the car.

Halloween · The hunt

Trap all four yard ghosts with G for 15 credits each. Defeat the scarecrow to open the cabin, then defeat the ghost, take its key, and escape by car.

After the maze · Weather trials

Escape the regular maze once to unlock Tornado Run and Tsunami Escape. Collect supplies while avoiding the funnel, or outrun the wave to reach high ground.
